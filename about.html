About Us

Welcome to Michigan Bar & Grill

Our Story

More than a restaurant

Nestled in the heart of our vibrant community, we pride ourselves on being more than just a restaurant; we're a place where memories are made and palates are pleased.

At Michigan Bar & Grill, our menu is a celebration of diversity and flavour. Start your day with our hearty and wholesome breakfast options, perfect for fueling your morning adventures. As the day unfolds, indulge in our carefully crafted lunch and dinner selections — from sizzling BBQ favourites to an array of mouth-watering burgers, each dish prepared with the freshest ingredients and an unwavering commitment to quality.

On The Menu

A little something for everyone

Our non-veg starters are a must-try, offering a tantalizing prelude to an unforgettable meal. For those who crave variety, our sides provide the perfect complement to any main course, ensuring a balanced and satisfying dining experience.

Understanding the fast-paced nature of modern life, Michigan Bar & Grill also offers convenient take-out options, allowing you to enjoy our culinary creations in the comfort of your home. Our catering service is here to elevate your special events, be it a family gathering, a corporate event, or a festive celebration.

The Ambiance

Contemporary chic, traditional warmth

The ambiance at Michigan Bar & Grill is designed to be welcoming and relaxed — perfect for a casual meal with friends, a romantic dinner, or a celebratory feast. Join us where every meal is an opportunity to unwind, indulge, and enjoy the finer things in life.
