About Us

Comfort food with a touch of elegance.

Nestled in the heart of Windsor, Michigan Bar & Grill is more than a restaurant — it's a place where memories are made and palates are pleased. Our menu celebrates diversity and flavour, from hearty breakfasts to sizzling BBQ favourites and mouth-watering burgers, each dish prepared with the freshest ingredients.

The ambiance is designed to be welcoming and relaxed: a blend of contemporary chic and traditional warmth, perfect for a casual meal with friends, a romantic dinner, or a celebratory feast.
